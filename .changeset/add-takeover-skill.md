Add the `takeover` skill (productivity bucket, user-invoked), imported from [awangs1986/takeover](https://github.com/awangs1986/takeover). It is the counterpart to `handoff`: where `handoff` is written by the outgoing session, `takeover` is reconstructed by the incoming one, from a conversation ID, a readable export, a URL, or a handoff file, and needs nothing from the old session (which may be out of quota, crashed, or in another tool). It indexes the records before reading them, rebuilds a short working summary with the goal, constraints, turning points, stopping point and next step, maps old project roots onto the current one with verification, describes the project in at most ten sentences, and asks for one confirmation (ten questions is the ceiling across the whole takeover, not a target). Corrections are handled in place with a revised SPEC shown before it is adopted. Before confirmation it is read-only. Wired into the `vibe` handbook as the third session-seam move beside `refocus` and `handoff`, into `ask-matt`'s phase-boundary map, and into the `vibe` signals table.
